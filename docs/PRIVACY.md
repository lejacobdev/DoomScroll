# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-11_

DoomScroll does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal
data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
SDK. The developer receives nothing from your use of the app.

## What stays on your devices

- **Motion data** from your Apple Watch is processed on the Watch to detect
  flicks and tilts. Only the resulting scroll commands (a pair of numbers)
  leave the Watch, and only to your own paired Mac or iPhone.
- **Pairing key.** When you scan the QR code on your Mac, a random secret is
  stored in the Keychain on your Mac, iPhone and Watch. Every message is
  authenticated with it, so other devices on your network cannot control
  your Mac.
- **Settings** (sensitivity, direction, target) are stored on your devices.

## Permissions and why

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera (iPhone) | Only to scan the pairing QR code. Images are never saved or sent. |
| Local Network (all) | To find and talk to your Mac on your own Wi-Fi. Traffic never leaves your network. |
| Accessibility (Mac) | So the Mac app can scroll other apps. macOS requires this for any app that does so. |
| Health / workout session (Watch) | A workout session is started only to keep the app running while your wrist is down. No health data is read and no workout is saved. |

## Network

DoomScroll communicates only between your own devices on your local
network. It makes no internet connections.
